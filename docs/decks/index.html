65 seconds. The dominant hypothesis is BUB1B. Established BUB1B–MVA1 biology links impaired BUBR1, checkpoint dysfunction, and chromosome-segregation errors. Here, two reported BUB1B candidates create a plausible case-level chain, not a diagnosis. They need orthogonal confirmation and phasing to establish whether they are on opposite alleles, and p.Asn1002Lys has no measured effect. Separately, SLC34A1 could relate to phosphate reabsorption, phosphate/calcium dysregulation, and renal findings. Its significance and biochemical evidence remain unresolved; it must not be presented as causing MVA. These hypotheses have distinct validation paths.

Therapeutic boundary · 25 seconds

One conditional therapeutic lead; no established corrective MVA drug

Conditional renal phosphate lead

Oral phosphate is relevant only if an SLC34A1-related renal phosphate-handling disease is independently confirmed.

An observational pediatric series reported urinary-calcium normalization in 10/11; this is not MVA benefit and not a treatment recommendation.

Required next evidence: renal genetic confirmation plus biochemical assessment.

TTK / MPS1 inhibitors

Not prioritized for corrective MVA therapy: cancer-directed mitotic disruption does not demonstrate checkpoint restoration.

PLK1 inhibitors

Not prioritized for corrective MVA therapy: oncology evidence does not establish benefit or safety in constitutional MVA.

No established disease-modifying MVA therapy identified. Confirm variants and phase; complete renal genetic and biochemical assessment.

Primary report · Turan et al. · repository · report04 / 04
25 seconds. Oral phosphate is a conditional lead only for independently confirmed SLC34A1-related renal phosphate-handling disease. The pediatric observational series reported urinary-calcium normalization in ten of eleven, not MVA benefit or a treatment recommendation. TTK or MPS1 and PLK1 inhibitors disrupt mitosis in cancer settings; they do not restore a constitutional checkpoint defect. No established disease-modifying MVA therapy was identified. Next: confirm and phase variants, then complete renal genetic and biochemical assessment.
