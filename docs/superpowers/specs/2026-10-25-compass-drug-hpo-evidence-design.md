# Compass drug–HPO evidence enrichment: design

## Purpose and boundary

Produce an auditable, explainable ranking of the 65 drug–target rows in the existing exploratory Compass `hsa05170` branch. Allow evidence-qualified **automatic promotion into the existing `decision_ready` filter** (`DECISION=TEST_FIRST`) by either a mechanism-directed route or a phenotype-directed route. This is research prioritization, not treatment advice or a claim of MVA efficacy. The unrelated 27-pathway literature-review inventory, also described as 65 agents, is not an input or a substitute for these rows. Do not widen pathway selection, alter DAVID thresholds, or rerun DAVID.

The input HPO list is a user-provided, reviewed, non-ignored code-only copy of `data/input/HPO.txt`, one `HP:nnnnnnn` per line, proposed at `docs/reports/hpo-codes.txt`. The ignored original is never opened by repository automation. Validate codes, reject malformed lines, deduplicate in stable order, resolve official labels and synonyms using an ontology source, and record the code-list and ontology-version hashes. No patient phenotype narrative is an input or report output.

Success: each original row retains its identity, pathway provenance and original decision; every automatically promoted row identifies its route, qualifying source IDs, observable benefit or directional evidence, safety/conflict assessment, rank components and remaining unknowns. Failed or incomplete retrieval is explicit. A repeat run with identical inputs/source snapshots produces identical assessments and provenance hashes.

## Architecture and data flow

1. **Snapshot:** take the existing `hsa05170` Compass `target_drug_review.csv`, target rationale, membership and provenance as an immutable input set; verify file hashes and that row identities and selected term correspond to this branch. The 65 count describes the current run, not a hard-coded limit. Preserve duplicates across distinct drug–target–pathway rows. Resolve active ingredient, product and combination regimen identities; share retrieval by canonical identity, but never attribute a regimen outcome to one component without evidence.
2. **Discover:** use HGNC/ChEMBL/Open Targets identities already captured by Compass; resolve HPO labels and synonyms. Search PubMed and Europe PMC with logged drug/alias × HPO queries and targeted drug–target/action queries. Query FDA product/indication labels for warnings and ClinicalTrials.gov for status and reported outcomes. MCP tools may aid research, but production decisions must be based on replayable saved public-source records with exact query, pagination, dates, source ID/URL and response hash. This is a targeted review, not a PRISMA-complete systematic review.
3. **Extract:** normalized evidence claims carry an immutable evidence ID, drug/regimen ID, target or HPO code, relationship type (benefit, worsening, mechanism, action, warning, contradiction), observed versus inferred flag, outcome direction, model/population, intervention/comparator, dose/time when available, source type, PMID/DOI/label/trial ID, passage locator, checked date and limitations. Extraction can propose claims, but a positive claim is ineligible without resolved identity, a matched source, a locatable passage and a recorded outcome/context. Search snippets, review articles, trial registration without results, pathway membership and FDA approval for another indication cannot establish benefit. Evidence with ambiguous drug attribution or incompatible phenotype remains contextual, not qualifying. Explicit negative findings stay in the ledger.
4. **Assess:** separate a mechanism-directed assessment from a drug × HPO phenotype assessment. Pharmacologic action, pathway relevance, HPO outcome, regulatory status, safety and trial status are distinct fields; one does not silently fill another. A source-completeness ledger distinguishes `not_searched`, `retrieval_failed`, `screening_pending`, `reviewed_no_qualifying_evidence` and `evidence_coded`. Relevant contradiction or missing semantic context yields `HOLD`, not a synthetic zero.
5. **Decide and export:** a single evaluator combines those assessments with the original Compass rationale to produce route-qualified `TEST_FIRST`, unchanged `HOLD`, or evidence-grounded `REJECT`; preserve the original decision and reason codes alongside new ones. Do not relax the existing stronger `ADVANCE_RESEARCH` gate. Export a normalized public-source evidence ledger, drug × HPO assessments, one row per original drug–target row with route and rank, source-status/provenance, and a research-only summary. External immutable/public source caches and passages stay outside Git; only reviewed, data-blind summaries, identifiers, aggregate metrics, hashes and statuses may be published under `docs/reports/<run-name>/` after checking repository policy. Do not persist prohibited patient-level fields or phenotype narratives in reports.

## Promotion contract

A **mechanism-directed** `TEST_FIRST` requires: resolved drug and exact target linkage; a cited action direction; a cited disease-state direction or directionally interpretable perturbation in applicable biology; compatible intervention; and a relevant observed experimental or clinical effect. Pathway membership supports relevance but cannot supply disease direction, effect or rescue. A direct MVA rescue study and named MVA-specific model are not mandatory. Mechanistic plausibility alone is insufficient. Existing `assess_target_action` remains the directional evaluator for this route.

A **phenotype-directed** `TEST_FIRST` requires: resolved drug or explicitly attributed combination regimen; a verifiable primary study reporting observed benefit for one of the supplied HPO codes or a justified, explicitly mapped close clinical equivalent; extractable outcome and population/model; and a recorded applicability assessment. A credible, attributable single case report can qualify with low priority; an animal/model observation is labelled preclinical and lower than comparable human evidence. Missing target direction is allowed **only on this route** and is shown as `UNKNOWN`; the decision is labelled symptomatic/phenotype-directed, never pathway correction. Mechanistic plausibility without observed phenotype benefit is contextual, not qualifying.

For either route, unresolved ingredient or regimen attribution prevents promotion. A documented applicable contraindication/serious worsening signal prevents promotion (with `REJECT` only when relevance to the proposed use is established); a known opposing action prevents mechanism-route promotion, and cannot be ignored when assessing phenotype-route risks. Materially contradictory outcomes or uncertain hazard applicability result in `HOLD` pending adjudication, not score averaging. Unknown safety is permitted for `TEST_FIRST` only as explicit `SAFETY_UNASSESSED`, never as a declaration of safety; it lowers priority. FDA approval, trial phase, article count and absence of cited harm cannot themselves promote. `TEST_FIRST` means research follow-up, not clinical use.

## Explainable priority

Eligibility is route-specific and evaluated **before** ranking. Use explicit ordinal priority bands, not a weighted sum of weak signals or a count of HPO matches. Within an eligible route, display independently: evidence directness/design (human controlled, human uncontrolled, case report, relevant animal/model), outcome relevance and applicability, independent replication, mechanism/action fit, contradictory evidence, safety completeness and source completeness. Human evidence is generally higher than model evidence at comparable applicability; an inapplicable human population does not automatically outrank an applicable model. Where a component is not established, label it `unknown`; do not encode unknown as zero or favorable. Rank by preset band then these displayed components with stable row ID as tie-breaker. Show both routes when both qualify, with their independent evidence IDs; do not combine a weak mechanistic hypothesis with an unrelated phenotype signal to manufacture a stronger causal claim. `ADVANCE_RESEARCH` retains the existing stronger reviewed rescue/model/safety standard.

Use three ordered bands for eligible rows: **A** = applicable, independently replicated human intervention benefit; **B** = applicable human intervention observation without independent replication, including a documented case report; **C** = applicable preclinical observed effect with a matching intervention. Mechanism-route evidence with no phenotype outcome uses the same bands based on its observed target/pathway effect and explicitly says `mechanism_only`; no effect observation means ineligible. Within a band sort first by applicability (`direct` before `analogous`), then independent replication, then directional fit (`matched` before `unknown` on the phenotype route only), then safety completeness (`assessed` before `unassessed`), then source completeness, and finally stable row ID. No single score substitutes for those visible dimensions.

## Failure handling and verification

Missing HPO file, malformed code, changed input hash, unresolved branch identity or malformed source record fails early with a status that names the offending non-sensitive input. Per-source timeouts and incomplete pages remain explicit, resumable and non-qualifying; no success is inferred from silence. Claims based on retracted or mismatched articles are invalid. Conflicting claims retain both evidence IDs and applicability contexts; automatic adjudication must not erase either. Version extraction rules and record any machine-assisted extraction separately from human review.

Synthetic regression cases cover each route, a single case report, model-only evidence, plausibility-only evidence, unknown direction on the phenotype route, opposite action, applicable and inapplicable label hazards, contradictory outcomes, combination attribution, missing safety, retrieval failure and stable ranking. A data-blind smoke replay checks input/output hashes, row preservation, emitted source completeness and aggregate transition counts without displaying patient-level values. The checked-in report explicitly says targeted evidence acquisition is not a systematic review and that automated decisions are not medical recommendations.

## Scope and prerequisite

Implement only the 65-row Compass branch and a dynamic HPO code list. Full 27-pathway discovery, meta-analysis, affordability, patient-specific matching and clinical recommendations are outside scope. The user must supply the approved code-only HPO copy at a non-ignored path before a real HPO replay; design and synthetic validation do not require access to the ignored original. This spec requires separate user review and approval before writing the implementation plan.
